# ROMATTIE Brand Asset Rules

## 1. Asset Classes

### FIXED MASTER ASSETS
These assets must be preserved exactly:
- ROMATTIE logo
- ROMATTIE product photos
- ROMATTIE product packaging
- ROMATTIE product cutout PNGs
- ROMATTIE character master files
- Official brand colors

Rules:
- Do not redraw
- Do not regenerate
- Do not restyle
- Do not recolor
- Do not distort
- Do not reinterpret
- Do not replace
- Do not change logo, shape, cap, label, proportions, material, illustration, or typography

Allowed:
- Place into new scenes
- Composite onto backgrounds
- Adjust shadow to match the scene
- Adjust lighting only if product identity remains unchanged
- Crop only with approval

### REFERENCE ONLY ASSETS
Reference assets are for mood, lighting, composition, humor, texture, and styling.
Do not copy them directly.
Do not let reference assets override ROMATTIE fixed brand assets.

### EDITABLE ASSETS
Backgrounds, props, textures, stickers, graphics, captions, and layouts may be modified.

## 2. Product Fidelity Rule

The product must remain recognizable and identical to the approved master product asset.
If product fidelity matters, use the fixed master product image or cutout PNG directly instead of generating a new product.

## 3. ROMATTIE Brand Tone

ROMATTIE content should be:
- Premium
- Sensory
- Touch-oriented
- Funny but not cheap
- Korean Instagram-friendly
- Mint and red aware
- Emotionally memorable

## 4. Forbidden

- Cheap meme style
- Random luxury cliché
- Changing product color
- Changing logo
- Inventing new packaging
- Overcrowding the product
- Making product look fake or low quality
- Replacing the real product with an AI-generated imitation

## 5. Future Content Production Rule

For staged content:
1. Generate or design the background, props, and scene.
2. Use the ROMATTIE product image as a fixed object.
3. Do not redraw the product.
4. Product identity must be checked before final delivery.
